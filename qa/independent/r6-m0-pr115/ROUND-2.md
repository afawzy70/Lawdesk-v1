## 3. Security architecture verdict and M1 gate

**Security architecture: NOT READY.** `adminInherited=false` is a useful start, but it is not a complete authority boundary while `role_permission` can feed sensitive codes into `account.permissions`, the ADMIN role invariant demands all known permissions, and first-custodian bootstrap has no specified controlled path. Require a single source of truth for sensitive grants, including a loader that rejects or filters sensitive role rows, explicit `RoleAdministration`/`UserAdministration` behavior, database constraints where practical, an owner-approved bootstrap record, delegation chain validation and real ADMIN integration tests. Distinguish the principal's next-request refresh from in-flight transaction checks.

For complaints, RESTRICTED creation and manifest transfer address much of the prior exposure. The existing `/cases/{id}/confidentiality` route is a direct declassification path. The design must require that it checks the complaint link and refuses a change to NORMAL without the same complaint authority and D-20-approved policy. Prove this through case APIs, search, reports, worklists, counts, timeline, attachment/download, audit, alerts, reverse link, retry and rollback tests. The transfer manifest must be approved by an in-scope checker under the owner-approved policy; the policy itself is still pending.

**M1 readiness: NO.** M1's proposed draft-only scope is a safer dependency order, but findings QA-001, QA-004, QA-005 and QA-006 affect its authority, schema and legal gate boundary. A draft capture endpoint must not leave legacy `final`, deadline or pointer mutations able to imply R6 finality or produce an unapproved effective due date. M1 cannot be self-authorized by the M0 documents; owner/CITRA Legal Affairs choices remain separate.
